 # Student Mental Health Analysis (Python)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **student mental health patterns** using a real-world dataset containing demographic, academic, and mental health–related information.  
The goal is to explore factors associated with **depression, anxiety, panic attacks**, and **treatment-seeking behavior** among students.

The analysis uses **Python exploratory data analysis (EDA)** techniques to uncover trends and correlations that can inform awareness and intervention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the prevalence of mental health issues among students
- Examine mental health differences by **gender**, **age**, **course**, and **CGPA**
- Identify patterns in **treatment-seeking behavior**
- Explore correlations between age, gender, and mental health conditions

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 1️⃣ Age Distribution
- Mean age: **20.5 years**
- Minimum age: **18**
- Maximum age: **24**
- Age was the only numerical variable in the dataset

---

### 2️⃣ Gender Disparities in Mental Health
- **Females reported higher cases** of:
  - Depression
  - Anxiety
  - Panic attacks
- This suggests a potential gender-based difference in mental health experiences among students.

---

### 3️⃣ Age and Mental Health Trends
- Depression remains relatively steady across ages
- Anxiety is more prevalent in early twenties and declines afterward
- Panic attacks are more common at ages **18–19**, then decline before slightly increasing again

---

### 4️⃣ Course and Mental Health
- Mental health issues vary by academic course
- The top 6 courses by student count were analyzed
- Some courses showed higher occurrences of depression, anxiety, or panic attacks

---

### 5️⃣ CGPA and Mental Health
- Students with **CGPA 3.00–3.49** showed higher levels of depression
- Students with **CGPA 3.50–4.00** showed higher levels of anxiety and panic attacks

---

### 6️⃣ Prevalence of Mental Health Issues
- Depression, anxiety, and panic attacks were all present at notable levels
- Visualization highlights the overall burden of mental health challenges among students

---

### 7️⃣ Treatment-Seeking Behavior
- Gender-based differences observed in seeking professional help
- A significant number of students experiencing mental health challenges did **not** seek specialist treatment

---

### 8️⃣ Correlation Analysis
- Slight negative correlations observed between:
  - Age and mental health issues
  - Gender and mental health indicators
- Suggests that mental health challenges may reduce slightly with age. This could be due to factors such as increased resilience, coping mechanisms developed over time, or changes in life circumstances.

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Mental health challenges are **common among students**
- Female students reported higher mental health issues
- Academic performance (CGPA) shows association with mental health conditions
- Many students experiencing mental health challenges do not seek treatment
